Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 3, 2026

At Zeetech Technical Services, we are committed to providing high-quality services and ensuring customer satisfaction. If you are not completely satisfied with our services, we offer a fair and transparent refund policy. Please read this policy carefully to understand your rights and responsibilities.

1. When Are You Eligible for a Refund?

You are eligible for a refund in the following situations:

  • Service Not Performed: If we fail to perform the service you booked and paid for, you are entitled to a full refund.
  • Service Partially Completed: If the service is only partially completed, you will receive a partial refund based on the work that was actually performed.
  • Service Not Satisfactory: If our service does not meet the agreed-upon standards and the issue cannot be resolved by re-inspection or rework, you may be eligible for a full or partial refund.
  • Technical Issue Cannot Be Resolved: If our technicians are unable to resolve the technical issue after multiple attempts, you are entitled to a full refund.

Important Note: Refunds are processed only after we have attempted to resolve the issue through our standard service procedures. We will always try to fix the problem first before processing a refund.

2. When Are You NOT Eligible for a Refund?

Refunds are not provided in the following cases:

  • If you change your mind after the service has been performed.
  • If the issue is caused by factors outside our control (e.g., power outages, weather conditions, building infrastructure).
  • If the service was performed according to the agreed specifications but you are simply unsatisfied without a valid technical reason.
  • If you cancel within less than 24 hours of the scheduled service (cancellation fees apply).
  • If the equipment or parts used were provided by you and were faulty or incompatible.

4. Refund Timelines

  • Eligibility Review: We review all refund requests within 24-48 hours of receiving them.
  • Refund Approval: Once approved, refunds are processed immediately.
  • Processing Time: Bank processing time is typically 5-7 business days depending on your bank/payment provider.
  • Cash Payments: Cash refunds are processed within 24 hours of approval.

Quick Tip: For faster processing, please have your booking reference number ready when you contact us. This helps us locate your record quickly.

5. Cancellation and Refund

If you need to cancel your booking, please note the following:

  • 24+ hours before service: No charges — full refund available.
  • Less than 24 hours before service: Up to 50% cancellation fee may apply.
  • Emergency cancellations: Contact us for waiver of cancellation fee.
